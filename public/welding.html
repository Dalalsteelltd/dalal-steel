📋 Batch Entry Tips

Type each Beam UID followed by the quantity welded with these parameters:

BM-4412-A(3)
BM-4500 x 2
BM-4501

The parser will expand to 6 units across 3 UID groups.

⚙️ Parameter Tip

Cross-check Heat Input ≈ (60 × A × V) / Speed (mm/min). Stay inside the WPS envelope.
